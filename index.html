🔔 What's the Beeping Sound?

The beep helps you keep a steady lifting cadence:

  1. Beep 1: Start lifting (4 seconds up)
  2. Beep 2: Start lowering (4 seconds down)
  3. Repeat until muscle failure

This slow 4-up / 4-down tempo is ideal for high-intensity training. Adjust the Beep Volume slider on the main menu, or set it to 0% to turn it off.

💪 How to Work Out

  1. Pick a workout (or tap 🔥 P90X)
  2. Choose your starting exercise
  3. Enter your weight and reps (your last numbers load automatically)
  4. Lift to the beep cadence until failure
  5. Tap Complete Set ✓
  6. Rest, or tap any remaining exercise to jump to it

The workout finishes only when every exercise is done — skipped ones circle back.

🔆 Keep Screen On

Leave this ON so your screen doesn't sleep mid-workout and cut off the chirp. If your screen ever does sleep, just unlock it — the chirp picks back up automatically.

🔥 P90X Workouts

P90X workouts run continuously with no rest timer and no chirp, following the exercise order from the videos.

🏋️ Equipment Types

Machine and Free Weight — more weight is better. Free weights step by 1 lb under 15, then by 5.

Bodyweight — no weight to enter. Push-ups, pull-ups, dips. You beat it by doing more reps.

Assisted — for machines that counterbalance you, like an assisted chin-up. The weight you add makes it easier, so less assist is better. The app knows this and tracks you in the right direction: dropping from 100 lbs to 90 lbs of assist counts as a PR.

🔴 Overload

Overload workouts run silent — no chirp — and add a rep table showing your best result at every rep count. Tap any row to load it as your target.

The Next Target card uses double progression: climb reps to the top of your range, then add weight (or drop assist) and reset. Set your rep range under Progression Settings.
