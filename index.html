🔔 What's the Beeping Sound?

The beeping sound helps you maintain proper exercise cadence:

  1. Beep 1: Start lifting (4 seconds up - concentric)
  2. Beep 2: Start lowering (4 seconds down - eccentric)
  3. Repeat until failure

This 4-second up / 4-second down tempo is ideal for high-intensity training and muscle growth.

Control the volume with the Beep Volume slider on the main menu. Set it to 0% to turn it off.

💪 How to Workout

  1. Pick a workout (Full Body, Upper Body, etc.)
  2. Select your equipment: Machine or Free Weight
  3. Enter your weight and reps goal
  4. Start the exercise:
    • Beep: Start lifting (4 seconds up)
    • Beep: Start lowering (4 seconds down)
    • Repeat with the beep pace until failure
  5. When done, tap "Complete Set"
  6. Rest between exercises
  7. Move to next exercise

📝 Free Weight Tips

When using free weights, weight increases by 1 lb each click from 0-14 lbs, then 5 lb increments after 15 lbs.
